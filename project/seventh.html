The Seventh Peak

UX/UI Design

A UX/UI Case Study & Interface Design Concept

Role:

Deliverables:

UI Design, UX Design, Prototyping, Concept Art, Graphic Design

Clients:

Personal Project

Tools Used:

Adobe Photoshop,
Illustrator, Figma, Blender

The Seventh Peak is a 3D action game concept where you play as a climber trying to prove to yourself that you're still good at it after a tragic event happened in your life. Game's main goal is to climb seven challenging mountains. The genre of the game is action - platform, where you mix those genres to progress through the levels (mountains). The game is set in a modern era and the style is rough and dull.
